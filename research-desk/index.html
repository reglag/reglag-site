# Research Desk The Research Desk is RegLag’s internal system for tracking **beliefs about regulatory, liquidity, and balance-sheet constraints** — and how those beliefs change (or don’t) over time. It is not a news feed or a monitoring dashboard. It records **judgment under pressure**. --- ## Current Objects ### RD-1 — Enforcement as a Control Surface **Status:** Baseline **Last belief change:** January 13, 2026 **Last reviewed:** Ongoing Tracks how enforcement operates forward through remedies, disclosure pressure, gatekeeper behavior, and silence — rather than backward through penalties alone. --- ### RD-2 — Liquidity Reallocation & Plumbing **Status:** Stable **Last belief change:** January 18–19, 2026 **Last reviewed:** Ongoing (daily) Tracks where U.S. dollar liquidity reallocates after leaving deposits, how it is absorbed, and where strain accumulates when balance-sheet capacity binds. Includes: - RD-2A: System-level absorption capacity - RD-2B: Treasury market absorption & dealer balance sheets --- ### RD-3 — Balance-Sheet Capacity & Intermediation Limits **Status:** Not yet activated **Last belief change:** — **Last reviewed:** — Will track when intermediation capacity (capital, leverage, internal limits, stress-test shadow constraints) becomes binding independent of asset risk. --- ### RD-4 — Credibility, Signaling & Institutional Belief **Status:** Not yet activated **Last belief change:** — **Last reviewed:** — Will track how institutional posture, signaling, and silence shape constraints when mechanical action is limited. --- ### RD-5 — Constraint Migration & Shadow Regulation **Status:** Dormant **Last belief change:** — **Last reviewed:** — Will track when constraints migrate outside formal regulation into private credit, contractual regimes, or physical systems. Activated only when evidence forces it. --- ## How the Desk Operates - **Daily:** Pre-committed tripwires are reviewed (silent by default). - **Weekly (early phase):** Proof-of-Work reviews may be published to show evidence checked. - **Belief updates:** Issued only when evidence forces a change. Silence is an outcome, not an absence of work. --- ## What the Desk Is Not - Not a dashboard - Not real-time monitoring - Not predictive or advisory - Not exhaustive The Desk exists to preserve **continuity, restraint, and memory**. --- _Last updated automatically as beliefs change or are reviewed._
