### Climate Policy: Rewriting the Legal Baseline

The administration’s move to repeal the EPA’s endangerment finding is not simply environmental positioning. It is administrative law with balance sheet consequences. The finding underpins federal greenhouse gas regulation under the Clean Air Act. Removing it would constrain the executive branch’s ability to regulate carbon emissions absent new legislation. Implementation will be slow and litigated. But capital models in utilities, energy, industrials, and infrastructure now must consider a narrower federal enforcement perimeter and a greater role for states and private actors. This is regulatory rollback by statutory reinterpretation rather than congressional rewrite.
